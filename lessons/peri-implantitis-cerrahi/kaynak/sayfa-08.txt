3.7 | Systemic antibiotics
Considering the bacterial etiology of peri-­implantitis, systemic
antibiotics have been investigated as adjunctive therapy in
combination with surgical management. The rationale is that
reducing the bacterial load systemically could enhance the effect
of mechanical debridement and implant surface decontamination
